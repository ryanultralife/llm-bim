# Machine / skid general arrangement

Wall-less equipment packs (MineClean, Proto-10, field skids) emit a Sierra
Star–style GA set automatically. Do not draw colored-box matplotlib sheets
as the issued plan.

**Law:** `docs/MACHINE_ENGINEERING_BAR.md` — the GA is a **cut of a connected
machine**, not a picture of envelopes. Headers/trays land both ends; fittings
sit at every bend; hardware is real `kind`s; the sheet title is the human
product name.

## What you get

`export_deliverables(mode="part")` or `export_construction_set` on a model
with equipment and **no walls**:

| Sheet | Content |
|-------|---------|
| G-001 | Cover + drawing list + `model.meta["process_notes"]` |
| EQ-101 | Plan: grids, 3-tier dims, collapsed machine footprints, leader tags |
| EQ-201 | South + east elevations |
| EQ-301 | Longitudinal section |
| EQ-501 | Equipment schedule |

## Authoring

```python
p.add_grid("U", [x0, *bays, x1], labels=["1", "2", "3", "4", "5"])
p.add_grid("V", [y0, 0.0, y1], labels=["A", "B", "C"])
p.model.meta["process_notes"] = [
    "Process: …",
    "Duty / honesty line [EE].",
]
p.export_deliverables(out, mode="part", plan_level="Skid", plan_scale=0.035)
```

If you skip `add_grid`, EQ-101 synthesizes bay grids from equipment centers.

Every solid should carry `equipment=` (parent tag) + `part=` so collapse
and leaders work. Frame / canopy tags that fill the envelope render as a
dashed outline, not a filled box over the train.

## Do not

- Hybrid STEP + bay boxes in one PNG (`product_views` rule).
- Claim process function for legacy geometry the design freeze rejected.
- Hand-draw a GA that cannot be regenerated by re-cutting the model.
- Title the sheet with a P/N prefix (`MB-MCLEAN`) when the product is `MineClean`.
- Cut a plan of floating or diagonal services and call it a GA.
